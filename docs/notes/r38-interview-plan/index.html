Let the AI interview you before it plans your week

Ask an AI for a plan without context, and apparently you have free afternoons. The fix is to let it interview you first. This note is the three steps from the video and both prompts.

The three steps

  1. Paste the interview prompt. It asks one question at a time and waits for your answer.
  2. Answer with your real week: sleep, commitments, the hours you actually have.
  3. Paste the plan prompt: three goals and a weekly plan that uses only those hours, with a plan B for bad days.

Then, every Sunday, tell it how the week went and ask it to adjust.

What happened in my demo session

A demo persona on the free plans of ChatGPT and Claude, recorded on 2026-10-28, in Spanish.

  • Without the interview, ChatGPT's plan was: pick your 3 most important tasks, spend 15 minutes organizing them, book a 30 to 45 minute block to focus. Fine for anyone. Which is the problem.
  • With the interview, both asked one question at a time ("Question 1 of 8: how have you been sleeping lately?"), then wrote a five-point summary and asked what was wrong.
  • The plan prompt gave three goals (sleep 7 hours, 10 minutes of English a day, move 10 minutes three times a week), a weekly table that used only the free time from the interview (20 minutes before the baby wakes up, 30 minutes after 9 pm, 1 hour on Saturday) and a plan B for each day ("walk 5 minutes").

Cost (prices as of Nov 2026)

$0 on the free plans. Limits checked 2026-10-28: Claude's free plan allows up to 5 projects and its usage resets every 5 hours, with no fixed message count. ChatGPT's free plan includes projects and memory with limited messages, files and memory; OpenAI does not publish the number.

Try it

Both prompts ran in Spanish on 2026-10-28, on the free plans of Claude and ChatGPT. These are their English translations; the Spanish originals are at the end.

Prompt 1: the interview.

I want you to interview me to get to know me, and then help me plan my week.
Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer before moving on.
Ask at most 8 questions: about my sleep, my real free time, what I want to learn and my money habits (without asking for amounts).
If an answer is vague, ask me once more.
When you finish, write a five-point summary of what you learned and ask me if anything is wrong.

Prompt 2: the plan. Paste it in the same chat, after you confirm the summary.

Using the summary of my interview:
1. Suggest exactly three goals for the next four weeks, one sentence each.
2. For each goal, a first step of 15 minutes or less.
3. Make a weekly plan as a table: day, what I do, how long. Use only the free time I told you about.
4. For each day, a plan B if that day goes wrong.
5. End with three questions to review the plan on Sunday.

Done when: you have a table with one row per day, every row fits the free time you gave, and every day has a plan B.

Gotchas

  • Read the summary before you say yes. The plan is built only from it. If it got your free time wrong, every row of the table will be wrong too.
  • Waits are not errors. In the ChatGPT session one answer was still loading after 40 seconds. Wait for it before you send the prompt again.
  • Money without amounts. The prompt asks about money habits but tells it not to ask for figures. You don't need to give a chatbot your salary to plan a week.
  • The Sunday review is the step people skip. A plan nobody reviews is a wish with formatting.

En español: pídele a la IA que te entreviste antes de hacer tu plan. Tres pasos: pega el prompt de entrevista (una pregunta a la vez), responde con tu semana real, y pega el prompt del plan (tres metas, una tabla con solo tus horas libres y un plan B por día). Probado el 2026-10-28 en los planes gratis de Claude y ChatGPT. Costo: $0 (precios a nov 2026). Los prompts originales:

Quiero que me entrevistes para conocerme y después ayudarme a planear mi semana.
Hazme una sola pregunta a la vez y espera mi respuesta antes de seguir.
Haz como máximo 8 preguntas: sobre mi sueño, mi tiempo libre real, lo que quiero aprender y mis hábitos con el dinero (sin pedirme montos).
Si una respuesta es vaga, pregúntame una vez más.
Al terminar, escribe un resumen de lo que aprendiste en cinco puntos y pregúntame si algo está mal.

Con el resumen de mi entrevista:
1. Propón exactamente tres metas para las próximas cuatro semanas, una frase cada una.
2. Para cada meta, un primer paso de 15 minutos o menos.
3. Arma un plan semanal en una tabla: día, qué hago, cuánto tiempo. Usa solo el tiempo libre que te dije.
4. Para cada día, un plan B si ese día falla.
5. Termina con tres preguntas para revisar el plan el domingo.
